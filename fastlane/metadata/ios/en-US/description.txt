Nora puts Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky and more in one private, ad-free app. Stay logged in to several accounts at once, and follow people in your own timelines, often without logging in.

YOUR OWN FEED
• Watch accounts on X, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon and TikTok, subreddits, and Facebook pages and groups
• New posts in chronological timelines, with no algorithm
• Many sources are checked without logging in
• Photo and video gallery view
• Notifications for new posts
• Import your sources, export them as OPML

PRIVATE AND AD-FREE
• Block ads and trackers with EasyList and EasyPrivacy
• Remove tracking parameters from links
• Anonymous mode hides sign-in and open-in-app prompts
• Search public posts without signing in

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS
• Profiles with separate cookie storage, for several accounts on the same site
• Tabs to keep many pages open

DOWNLOAD AND MORE
• Save images and videos from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X
• Usage limits to spend less time scrolling
• Select text, zoom and custom CSS

SUPPORTED SITES
Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Reddit, Threads, TikTok, Tumblr, VK and X (Twitter).

Nora shows each site's own web pages, with ads and trackers blocked. It is open source, and also available for Linux, macOS and Windows.
